**Full description** (max 4000):

Pundo is a personal finance app built on real double-entry accounting, the same method accountants use. Encode the transactions from your bank and credit card statements, and get proper financial statements for your own money.

ENCODE LIKE A BANK REGISTER
• Pick a bank account or card and type each statement line: date, ref no., payee, account, payment or deposit
• The account can be anything: an expense, income, another bank, a credit card, a loan, an investment or a person who owes you
• Payees you've used before fill in their account automatically
• Split one payment across several categories
• Statement check: enter your statement's ending balance and see right away whether everything is encoded

ALL YOUR ACCOUNTS, YOUR TRUE NET WORTH
• Debit cards, bank and e-wallet accounts as cash; credit cards and loans as liabilities
• Investments, real estate, vehicles and other assets, revalued to market value whenever you like
• Net worth trend on the dashboard

WHO OWES YOU, AND WHOM YOU OWE
• Track receivables and payables person by person
• Record money lent, repayments, borrowings, and bills a friend paid for you
• Printable statement of account for each person

FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
• Income statement (profit & loss) with savings rate
• Balance sheet (statement of net worth)
• Cash flow statement: operating, investing and financing
• General ledger with running balances
• Print or save as PDF, or export to CSV, ready for a presentation

PERIOD END
• Revalue investments and assets, record depreciation and accruals
• Close a period to roll net income into retained earnings and lock it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account or sign-up required
• Your data stays on your device: no servers, no ads, no tracking
• Automatic backup to your Google account, restored when you set up a new phone
• Export a backup file to Google Drive or email whenever you like

Defaults to the Philippine peso (₱); you can switch to any currency.
