Privacy Policy — Pundo

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Summary

Pundo does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Accounts, transactions, people and settings you enter are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. There are no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Automatic backup (Android)

On Android, Pundo keeps a backup copy of your data inside the app's own storage. Android's built-in backup service may copy it to your own Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is controlled by your phone's backup settings (Settings → Google → Backup), is stored by Google under your account, and is never sent to or accessible by the developer.

Files you export

When you choose Export backup, Export CSV or Print / PDF, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (for example a folder, email or cloud drive) is your choice and is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. Choosing a file to import or restore uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the file you select.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase everything inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because no data is kept anywhere else, this removes it completely.

Children

The app is a general personal-finance tool and is not directed at children under 13.
