Privacy Policy — Pundo

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Summary

If you use Pundo without signing in, everything you enter stays on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. If you choose to sign in to sync across devices, your books are stored in your own private space in the cloud so they can appear on your other devices. We never sell or share your data, show ads or use it for anything else.

What the app stores

Accounts, transactions, people and settings you enter are saved in the app's local storage on your device. Unless you sign in to sync, nothing leaves your device. There is no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Sync and your account (optional)

When you create an account, we store your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account's basic profile (name, email, profile picture link); if you use a password it is stored only as a secure hash (all handled by Google Firebase Authentication), and your accounts, people, transactions and report settings (in Google Cloud Firestore, Singapore region). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Access rules allow only you, signed in with your account, to read or change your data. Your security code is never uploaded.

Sync subscription (optional)

Sync is a paid monthly subscription bought through Google Play; the rest of Pundo is free. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your card or payment details. To confirm your subscription, our server receives the Google Play purchase token and stores the subscription status (active or not, renewal date, product) with your account, plus a one-way fingerprint of the purchase token so a purchase can only be linked to one account. You can cancel anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; deleting your Pundo account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

You can delete your account and all synced data at any time in Settings → Sync across devices → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.

Automatic backup (Android)

On Android, Pundo keeps a backup copy of your data inside the app's own storage. Android's built-in backup service may copy it to your own Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is controlled by your phone's backup settings (Settings → Google → Backup), is stored by Google under your account, and is never sent to or accessible by the developer.

Files you export

When you choose Export backup, Export CSV or Print / PDF, the app creates a file and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (for example a folder, email or cloud drive) is your choice and is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions other than Google Play billing, used only when you choose to subscribe to sync. Choosing a file to import or restore uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the file you select.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase everything inside the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Because no data is kept anywhere else, this removes it completely.

Children

The app is a general personal-finance tool and is not directed at children under 13.
