Delete your Pundo account

Pundo — personal finance app by the developer listed on Google Play.

Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Pundo and go to Settings & backup.
  2. Under Sync across devices, tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your security code and your account password to confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your sign-in and all of your synced data stored in the cloud (accounts, people, transactions and settings). The books saved on your own device are not touched; you can erase them in Settings → Erase everything or by uninstalling the app.

Ask us to delete it

If you can no longer open the app, email prioloneil@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and ask for your Pundo account to be deleted. We delete the account and all associated cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing. Pundo keeps no copies of your data after deletion. People who never signed in have no data on our servers at all.
